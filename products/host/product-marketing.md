## Product Overview
**Brand architecture:** Hububb is "Everything around Stays," the parent brand. Three products sit under it, each marketed on its own: **Host** (this file), **Stay** (guest-facing bookings, a separate product), and **Work** (the service-provider app, a separate product). Never market or imply a sibling product's capabilities.

**Positioning (lead, from 14 Oct 2026):** Longer stays, with Hostie running the day-to-day. Hububb is the channel for longer stays: corporate and relocation guests book a month or more, so the calendar fills in blocks, and short stays on the host's other channels fill the gaps. Hostie is how it runs without the host: guests answered, cleans booked at checkout, prices tuned. Live site hero: "Get longer stays while your units run on autopilot."

**One-liner:** Longer stays anchor the year. Short stays fill the gaps.

**Value proposition:** Add longer stays to the listing you already have, and hand the day-to-day to Hostie. The listing stays where it is; the calendar fills in blocks; the admin is handled.

**Category:** The channel for longer stays, run by an AI host. Not a takeover, not a property manager, not another tool to drive.

**Supporting positioning:** Your autonomous AI host. Bookings, guests, cleaning, and pricing, all handled while you watch. Still true and still usable, now as the "how" behind longer stays rather than the headline.

**Live today:** longer stays of 28 nights or more from corporate and relocation demand (promoted through Hububb Stay), HomeHold (a guest in the property adds another month in one tap, at the agreed rate), unified inbox, one calendar across every channel (Airbnb, Booking.com, Vrbo, Expedia, Agoda, Trip, and more; a booking on any channel blocks the others within seconds), AI guest messaging agent (Hostie, answers automatically in the host's voice), direct booking website, dynamic pricing (short gaps priced so they are not left empty), smart access arrangements, automated cleaning (booked the moment a guest checks out), services on demand (vetted cleaners, handymen, locksmiths, electricians, booked from the app and billed at cost), listing import from an existing Airbnb, guest portal, and a performance dashboard. A professional team option exists for London stays over 28 nights (capped; do not lead with it).

## Target Audience
**Who they are:** Owner-operator short-term rental hosts, from the 1-property side-hustler to the roughly 20-unit operator.
**Where:** London first, then the UK, then phased global. London is the focus for now.
**Decision-maker:** The host. An owner-operator purchase, no committee.
**Primary use case:** Fill the calendar with longer stays (beyond what short lets and London's 90-night cap allow) while the day-to-day runs itself.

**Jobs to be done:**
- Earn from the whole year: longer stays anchor the calendar, short stays fill the gaps. (London caps entire-home short lets at 90 nights a year; see the legal hold under Objections before linking longer stays to the cap.)
- Fewer changeovers: one check-in for a month instead of a string of weekends.
- Have the property run itself: bookings, guest messages, cleaning, pricing, and access, all handled.
- Stay in control without doing the work: adjust what the AI knows and how it speaks.
- Earn more with less effort: higher earnings and fewer fires.
- Get out of the WhatsApp-and-spreadsheets chaos and the 9 a.m. group chats.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Short lets alone leave most of the year on the table and make the rest of it hard work. In London an entire home can only be short-let for 90 nights a year, and every short stay is another changeover, another round of messages, another price to set. Running it by hand quietly becomes a stressful, disconnected second job. The host is the operating system: chasing cleaners, living in WhatsApp and spreadsheets, putting out fires, fielding 9 a.m. group chats.

**Why alternatives fall short:** Most hosts run it manually, with disconnected setups: a spreadsheet, WhatsApp, one trusted cleaner, gut feel. Occasionally there is old, clunky PMS software, but that is a net of non-proprietary tools that are paid for separately and, even when they talk to each other, get lost in translation. The effort to make sense of it is not worth it.

**What it costs them:** Time (always on), money (silent leaks, revenue left on the table), mental load (the rental lives in their head), and risk (missed cleans, no-shows, bad reviews).

**Emotional tension:** The passive income that became a full-time job. What they want is to be in control without being involved in everything.

## Differentiation
Hububb brings the longer-stay demand with it. Corporate and relocation guests book a month or more through Hububb Stay, so the calendar fills in blocks; short stays on the host's existing channels fill the gaps, priced automatically. Most hosting tools manage the bookings you already get; Hububb adds a channel of longer ones and runs the day-to-day around both.

Hububb is also the interconnected app that combines cleaning, messaging, pricing, access arrangements, property guides, and more under one roof, all handled by an AI agent, so the host can rest instead of clicking through a complex UI. Every other option is a net of separate tools you pay for and stitch together; even when they speak to each other it gets lost in translation and the effort is not worth it. Here, everything is under one roof, properly automated by your AI host, and cleanly presented in a reports dashboard.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** The 90-night cap, empty midweeks, constant changeovers, manual chaos, the always-on second job.
**Pull:** Longer stays that fill the calendar in blocks, and a property that runs itself. Higher earnings and fewer fires, all handled while you watch.
**Habit:** My cleaner, my spreadsheet, my WhatsApp threads.
**Anxiety:** Trusting an AI with guests, pricing, and access. Answered by full control over what the agent knows and how it speaks.
