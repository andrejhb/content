# Rejected · all accounts

What Andrej (or the team) turned down, and the pattern behind it. `hububb-posts` reads this before every plan; a draft that repeats any of these gets rewritten before anyone sees it. Newest at the bottom.

Add entries by reacting to a plan ("3 is slop because..."). Entries marked `seed` were collected from earlier feedback on 14 Oct 2026.

Entry format:

```
## <slug>
`account` · `date` · `source`

**What it was:** ...
**Why (Andrej):** "<verbatim>"
**Pattern to avoid:** one generalised line
```

---

## ui-led-paid-cards
`host` · `Jul 2026` · `marketer static batch` · `seed`

**What it was:** a bare screenshot feature card, then a phone dashboard mockup over a lifestyle panel.
**Why (Andrej):** "needs to be so much better", asked for a "simple lifestyle one".
**Pattern to avoid:** leading with product UI when the post is not literally about the product. Lifestyle photo first; UI only as one proof device.

## decorative-gimmicks
`host` · `2026` · `hero creative` · `seed`

**What it was:** a sunrise arc and concentric circles motif, plus a top-right logo.
**Why (Andrej):** called it slop.
**Pattern to avoid:** decoration that does not prove the line. Restraint, real imagery, generous space.

## one-ai-scene
`general` · `Jul 2026` · `connected/fragmented carousel close` · `seed`

**What it was:** one continuous generated take blending host, guest, and cleaner into a single apartment.
**Why (Andrej):** read as too AI.
**Pattern to avoid:** a single fabricated scene standing in for several real places. Animate real photos separately and cross-dissolve.

## periods-on-image
`host, stay` · `Oct 2026` · `Paper Host Draft 1` · `seed`

**What it was:** on-image sentences ending in full stops.
**Why (team):** "a giveaway that it's AI generated".
**Pattern to avoid:** full stops at the end of on-image lines. Captions keep normal punctuation.

## blueground-lookalike
`host` · `13 Oct 2026` · `Paper p-9-0, Host 3` · `seed`

**What it was:** "We handle everything / you just watch / the bookings come in", woman in a blazer against a blue sky, [Connect your Airbnb].
**Why (team):** make it dusk or dawn, "not a direct copy of Blueground"; the copy should be about longer stays as a channel.
**Pattern to avoid:** copying a reference's surface (flat blue sky, navy pill, lockup) and generic "we handle everything" claims. Borrow the grammar, change the light and the place, say something only Hububb can.

## product-labels-on-frame
`host, stay` · `Oct 2026` · `Paper, every round` · `seed`

**What it was:** "Hububb Host", "Hububb Stay", "About" labels and footers on the artboard.
**Why (team):** removed every round; H icon only and subtle, or no logo.
**Pattern to avoid:** stacking brand labels on the frame.

## plain-text-heavy-explainer
`host` · `Oct 2026` · `Paper p-5-1, Explainer 2` · `seed`

**What it was:** a five-slide explainer that was mostly type.
**Why (Peter):** "plain and text heavy".
**Pattern to avoid:** explainers carried by paragraphs. One line per slide, the visual does the explaining.

## slow-heavy-motion
`all` · `Aug 2026` · `first three motion films` · `seed`

**What it was:** long holds, 700 to 900 font weights, an outro stacking wordmark, brand line, handle, and CTA.
**Why (Andrej):** too slow, fonts too bold, outro overloaded.
**Pattern to avoid:** beats longer than the read, weights above 600, anything but the logo in the outro.

## hostie-drafts-you-approve
`host` · `Jul and Oct 2026` · `marketer PDF, Paper p-9-0 card, Host caption 5` · `seed`

**What it was:** "Hostie drafted it. You approved from the sofa." / "AI-drafted replies. You review and send."
**Why (Andrej):** corrected twice: Hostie answers automatically, not just drafts.
**Pattern to avoid:** any draft-and-approve framing for Hostie.
