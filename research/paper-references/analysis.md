## 6. Team feedback themes (Andrej and Peter)

- **Punctuation:** no periods on image; they are "a giveaway that it's AI generated". Captions keep normal punctuation.
- **Hierarchy:** two-tone, line 2 at about 80 percent opacity or grey.
- **Type:** bigger text, semi-bold, headline and sub one size.
- **Logo:** remove "Hububb Host", "Hububb Stay", "About" labels; H icon only and subtle, or no logo.
- **Visuals:** must demonstrate the feature and match the copy (phone plus coffee, happy because the agent replied); something more visual on slide 1.
- **UI:** no broken or fake UI, mockups come from code; show a chat with a host; service-provider card; listing card for colour.
- **Format:** explainers wanted (Kindred, Casa); not plain or text-heavy; one slide per segment.
- **Contrast:** lighter greys; black high contrast praised; light and dark versions.
- **Originality:** avoid Blueground look-alikes; use dusk or dawn, not flat blue sky.
- **Positioning:** Host is "the channel for longer stays".
- **CTA:** black button, "List on Hububb".
- **Micro-copy:** "Staying longer?" instead of "Stay longer"; slide-order swaps.

## 7. Synthesis

- **Headline formulas:** contrast couplet, already-done moment ("Guest checked out / Clean already booked"), you/we role split, question, imperative benefit, tricolon, single proof stat, brand sign-off.
- **Word counts:** headlines 2 to 10 words, median about 5, two lines. Subheads 6 to 14 words, mechanism or proof only. Chips and labels 2 to 5 words.
- **Hook and payoff split:** within a slide, line 1 is the tension, line 2 (grey) is the payoff, the sub says how, a UI card or chip is the proof. Across slides: hook, mechanisms, resolve. The caption's first line re-voices the slide-1 headline.
- **CTA:** on image, a pill on the last slide or a single post only. In captions, "Link in bio." for Host; Stay ends on a tagline.
- **Carousel shapes:** problem, mechanism, resolve (3); split promise ("Longer stays" / "Shorter bookings to fill the gaps" / "Make room for longer stays"); you, we, brand; tension to resolve (4); how-it-works journey: book, arrive, live, extend (5); explainer opening on "What is Hububb?" (6); one slide per audience segment.
- **Visual archetypes:** full-bleed lifestyle with type in negative space; white band over a rounded photo; charcoal stage with framed imagery; shape mask; fanned stack; UI proof overlay; 2x2 tile grid; checklist rail; duration chips; sidebar scrim column; frosted card; dollhouse facade; sticky notes; type-only close.
- **Photo direction:** warm daylight or dusk, neutral cream and greige interiors, one relaxed subject with phone or coffee, London landmarks seen from behind.
- **Type:** one grotesk, sentence case, medium to semi-bold, grey second line. Colour comes from the photo, with white, charcoal and grey grounds.

## 8. QA flags in the drafts

- Hostie "drafted, you approved" (p-9-0 card, Host caption 5) contradicts the confirmed product truth that Hostie answers automatically.
- Typos: "Than" (Then), "wit h", "Mid - stay", "Move - in", "Hububb Stay is flexible and mid-term stays".
- Stray periods on image.
- "Set up in under" vs "Set up under".
- Claims needing sign-off before reuse: 90%+ (in Host qa.json), hundreds of cleans monthly (in Host qa.json, cleaning context only), 24/7 human support, no deposit, within minutes (not in any qa.json).
