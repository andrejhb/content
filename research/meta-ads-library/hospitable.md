## Analysis

### Core angles / pillars
1. **The hidden work behind every stay.** "Listing your place is the easy part. Then come the guest messages, calendar updates, and pricing decisions."
2. **Freedom moments.** Host is elsewhere, the system answers. "Answer check-in questions without putting down your paddle."
3. **Specific micro-chores, automated.** "Keep hot tub prep on schedule without texting your cleaner every time."
4. **Keep your tools, connect them.** "Connect your go-to tools instead of replacing them."
5. **Earn, not just organise.** "Choose your next PMS for what it helps you earn, not only what it helps you organize."
6. **Owner trust (marketplace).** "Your property is too valuable to hand over without doing your homework."

### Hook patterns
- Concrete scene or micro-task as line one: "without putting down your paddle", "hot tub prep", "copying check-in messages and texting cleaner reminders".
- Reframe: "A great listing needs more than great photos." / "Listing your place is the easy part."
- Internet idiom: "Looking for the green flags before choosing your hosting software?"
- Seasonal deadline: "before December check-ins begin", "Make the switch before the rush."

### Headline formulas
- Two-beat with full stops: "Fewer tools. Less daily admin." / "New Season. New PMS."
- Question on the operation: "Your property is ready. Are your operations?" / "Still putting off your PMS switch?"
- "X without the Y": "Guest replies without the scramble".
- "What if your PMS made you money?"

### Length and structure
- Medium: 3 to 5 short paragraphs separated by line breaks, 40 to 70 words. Hook line, mechanism line, a "Fewer X. Fewer Y." or "You focus on X." summary, then "Start for free." as its own line.
- No emoji at all. No exclamation marks. Hedged, accurate claims ("designed to help", "when available", "eligible").

### CTA labels
- See details (dominant), Learn More (owners). In-copy: "Start for free." On-image pills: "TRY FOR FREE", "SWIPE RIGHT", "FIND MY MATCH", "MAKE THE SWITCH", "YOUR LISTINGS, READY".

### Proof devices
- Compact triplet: "4.9-star reviews. 90k users. 300+ integrations." Plus partner badge "Airbnb Preferred+ Software Partner".
- Named integrations (PriceLabs, Turno, QuickBooks) as trust by association.

### Offer framing
- Free to start. Seasonal urgency is calm, framed as giving yourself time rather than a sale.

### Visual direction
- Warm, editorial, aspirational: golden-hour villas and cabins, a woman on a sofa, a laptop on a terrace. Serif italic accent words in raspberry against a clean sans. Native formats too: iPhone Notes list ("Green flags:"), comparison tables, before/after columns, carousel that walks an owner through a decision. UI appears as small cards, never full screens.
