## Analysis

### Core angles / pillars
1. **New demand channel (Google Vacation Rentals)** with "zero commission fees".
2. **Industry data and education** as the hook: reports, playbooks, webinars "straight from Airbnb".
3. **Cost of a single failure**: "a missed or delayed message can cost you a 5-star review", "A single double booking damages your reputation on every channel."
4. **Quantified automation**: "Automate up to 93% of guest messaging with AI", "Save your team 4-5 hours per day", "Earn 25% more per listing".
5. **Switch from the competitor**: "Switch from Guesty and unlock 44% more listings".
6. **Take revenue back from OTAs**: "You're handing 15-20% to OTAs on every booking."

### Hook patterns
- Role question: "Multi-property owner/manager?", "Managing multiple short-term rentals?"
- Consequence statement: "A single double booking damages your reputation on every channel."
- Stat tension: "AI adoption is up. Direct bookings? Still under 25% for most operators."
- Cost callout: "You're handing 15-20% to OTAs on every booking."
- Time-now: "Your guests are searching on Google right now."

### Headline formulas
- "Stop + painful habit": "Stop spending hours on owner statements at month end", "Stop leaving 25% of your revenue on the table".
- "Never + fear": "Never miss a guest message again".
- Number + result: "44% More Listings. Real Results."
- Clever reversal: "Double your bookings; avoid double bookings."
- Weak generic: "Take Your Bookings to New Heights", "Streamline Tasks, Delight Your Guests".

### Length and structure
- Two lengths: short role-question ads (1 to 2 sentences) and long pain-agitate-solve ads (2 paragraphs, 70 to 100 words, no emoji). Guides use emoji bullets (🎯 ✅ ⭐).

### CTA labels
- Contact Us (demo), Download (guides, reports), Book Now, Learn More, Sign Up (webinars).

### Proof devices
- Percentages: 93%, 25%, 44%, 4-5 hours per day, 15-20%.
- Partner names: Google, Airbnb, Booking.com, Marriott Bonvoy, Key Data.
- Case studies with before/after unit counts.

### Visual direction
- Clean, bright, flat. Cream or white backgrounds, orange gradient, one big number in display type, a single UI card (chat bubble, chart) floating. Report covers rendered in 3D for lead gen. Little photography.
