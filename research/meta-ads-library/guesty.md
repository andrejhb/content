## Analysis

### Core angles / pillars
1. **AI agents as a team.** "dozens of smart AI agents working 24/7 to clear your inbox, dispatch maintenance teams". Human in the loop: "they always loop you in for critical decisions".
2. **Revenue proof.** "+33% in their first year", "doubled revenue".
3. **Reviews and rankings.** "save the stay before it costs you reviews", "Stop letting missed messages tank your search rankings."
4. **Owner retention for PMCs.** "A messy PMS can cost you owners."
5. **Switching risk removed.** "test it out before you turn off your old software", "zero downtime".
6. **You are the bottleneck.** "Are you running your short-term rental business, or is it running you?"

### Hook patterns
- Open letter: "To the Airbnb host struggling to ...: your manual pricing strategy is broken."
- Role + fear question: "Vacation rental managers, worried about lost bookings when you switch?" / "Afraid to switch your PMS?"
- Customer + number: "Krain manages 140 vacation rentals."
- "Imagine having a 24/7 virtual team ..."
- Inversion question: "Are you running your business, or is it running you?"

### Headline formulas
- Triple "More": "More 5-star reviews. More bookings. More revenue."
- Stat headline: "Grow revenue an average +33% in year one".
- Imperative: "Stop Guessing Your Prices", "Automate Your Rentals".
- Metaphor: "The engine inside 5* guest experiences".

### Length and structure
- Long. Video ads often run 60 to 110 words in 4 to 6 paragraphs with fragments ("Every late checkout. Every pricing surge. Every fraudulent payment.") and a closing slogan ("The future is here. Book your demo today.").
- Checklist ads use ✅ without a space and end with "And so much more…".

### CTA labels
- Sign up, Learn more, See details. In-copy: "Book your demo today."

### Proof devices
- Scale: "500K+ properties", "10+ years of data", "60+ listing sites".
- Averages: "+33%". Named customers with unit counts.
- Superlatives that are not substantiated: "world's #1".

### Visual direction
- Mix: polished UI over architectural photography, before/after split, photographic metaphor (coffee-stained contract), UGC creator selfie, customer phone-in-hand testimonial with burned-in subtitles. Deep green and cream palette with serif italic accent words ("More *revenue*").

### Weak spots worth not copying
- Hype verbs: "exploded revenue", "revenue rocket", "The future is here."
- Generic checklists ("Grow revenue, Maximize occupancy, Receive more 5* reviews, And so much more…") that any competitor could run, and do.
